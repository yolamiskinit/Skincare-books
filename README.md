# My Skincare Books

A simple bookkeeping app for your skincare business (TikTok, eBay, Website).
Records sales, tracks stock, logs expenses, and shows your profit — installs on your phone like a real app, works without internet, and needs no monthly fees.

## Get it onto your phone (one-time setup, ~10 minutes, free)

You need a free GitHub account to host the app so your phone can open it.

1. Go to **github.com** and create a free account (if you don't have one).
2. Click the **+** in the top right → **New repository**. Name it `skincare-books`. Keep it **Public**. Click **Create repository**.
3. On the new repository page, click **Add file → Upload files**.
4. Open this folder on your computer (`Skincare Bookkeeping App`), select **all the files and the `icons` folder**, and drag them into the browser upload area. Click **Commit changes**.
5. In the repository, go to **Settings → Pages** (left sidebar).
6. Under "Build and deployment", set **Branch** to `main` and folder to `/ (root)`. Click **Save**.
7. Wait about a minute, then refresh — GitHub shows a link like `https://yourusername.github.io/skincare-books/`. That's your app's permanent web address.

## Install it on your phone

Open that link on your phone:

- **iPhone**: open the link in **Safari** → tap the Share icon (square with arrow) → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android**: open the link in **Chrome** → tap the menu (⋮) → **Add to Home screen** / **Install app**.

An app icon appears on your home screen. Tap it — it opens full-screen, no browser bars, just like a normal app.

## Very important: your data lives on your phone only

Everything you enter (sales, stock, expenses) is stored privately on your phone/browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere, nobody else can see it. That also means:

- **Back up regularly.** Open the app → tap the ⚙️ Settings icon → **Download Full Backup**. Save that file to your email or Google Drive/iCloud so you never lose your records if your phone is lost, reset, or you clear browser data.
- If you ever get a new phone, install the app there too, then use **Restore from backup file** in Settings to bring your data back.
- Use **Export Sales as CSV** any time you want your sales in Excel.

## How to use it day to day

1. **Stock tab** — add each product you sell once (name, cost price, selling price, how many you have). Categories match your product sheets (Serum & Treatment, Lotions, etc.).
2. **Sales tab** — every time you make a sale, tap **+**, pick the product, fill in the platform, price and fees. It works out what you earned and your profit automatically, and takes the item off your stock count for you.
3. **Expenses tab** — log subscriptions, stock purchases, shipping supplies, etc. as they happen.
4. **Home tab** — see this month's revenue, profit, expenses and net profit at a glance, plus low stock warnings and unpaid orders.
5. **Reports tab** — profit & loss by month/year, best-selling products, breakdown by platform (TikTok, eBay, Website...).

## Updating the app later

If you (or anyone helping you) improve the app's files, just repeat step 3–4 above (Upload files, Commit changes) in the same GitHub repository — your data on your phone is untouched, only the app itself updates.
